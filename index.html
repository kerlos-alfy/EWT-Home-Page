Ask us a question
What services are available at Eye Walk & Talk? +

Our services include speech therapy, orthoptics, occupational therapy, physiotherapy and psychology. Visit our services section to learn more about each one.

Do you support children and adults? +

The clinic describes its services as supporting children, adults and families. You can contact the team to discuss whether a particular service is suitable for your needs.

How do I request an appointment? +

Use the appointment request page or call the clinic directly. The team can follow up about availability and the next steps; submitting a request is not an automatic confirmation of a time.

Where is the clinic located? +

Eye Walk & Talk Clinic is located at Exit 41, Sheikh Zayed Road, Vakson Commercial Building, Office 116, Dubai.
